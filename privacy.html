Privacy

Last updated August 16, 2026

This page is short because there isn't much to disclose. 5 Star Tap is a small business that sells signs. It is not in the data business.

What I collect

When you order, Stripe's checkout collects what's needed to do the job and nothing else: your name, business name, email, phone, billing and shipping address, and the Google "Ask for reviews" link you want programmed onto your placard.

What it's used for

Programming your order, shipping or installing it, sending your receipt and invoice, and contacting you about your order. That's the whole list.

What never happens

  • Your card number never touches me. Payment runs entirely on Stripe; I see the last four digits on a receipt, like any shopkeeper.
  • Your data is never sold or rented, to anyone, for anything.
  • No marketing lists. You won't get a newsletter because you bought a sign.
  • No ad trackers and no analytics on this site. The pages are plain files — no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no cookies set by me.

Who else is involved

Three companies touch something when you use this site, each for one job: Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy; GitHub Pages hosts these files and keeps standard server logs; Google Fonts serves the two typefaces, which means Google sees the font request. I never need — and never ask for — your Google password or any access to your accounts.

Keeping and deleting

Order records live in Stripe, where tax law requires me to keep transaction history. If you want the details I hold about you, or want them deleted where the law doesn't force me to keep them, email and it will be done: Spindola.Software@gmail.com.
