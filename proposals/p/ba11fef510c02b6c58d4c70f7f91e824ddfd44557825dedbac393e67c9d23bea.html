One honest note

Getting the most out of them

The +38 at Sacramento came with zero staff involvement, and that is the number to plan against. But if you want more, an optional line at checkout — “tap the star if we did alright today” — moves the number more than anything else and costs nothing. Entirely your call; nothing in this proposal depends on it.

Worth noting for compliance: Google explicitly permits and encourages businesses to ask customers for reviews. The placards do not filter, screen, gate or incentivize — every customer sees the same link and writes whatever they want. If your teams do mention the placards, the ask stays neutral and nothing of value is ever offered in exchange for a review. That is what keeps the reviews counted and the listing in good standing.
